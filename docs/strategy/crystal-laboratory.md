# Crystal Laboratory

::: info Quick take
The Crystal Laboratory turns ordinary resources into Fire Crystals, and later turns Fire Crystals into Refined Fire Crystals. It arrives with the Fire Crystal Age, not at FC5 as older guides claim. The number that matters: **each refine returns 2.1 Fire Crystals on average**, but the first refine of the day costs 20,000 resources and the sixth costs 200,000. That is a **tenfold efficiency collapse** across a single day's slots. Do the cheap ones every day without fail. Do the expensive ones only when resources are genuinely spare.
:::

## When It Unlocks

The Crystal Laboratory appears when your city enters the **Fire Crystal Age** — Furnace 30, Fire Crystal Lv. 1. It does not wait for FC5.

::: warning Published gating does not match observation
Community sources widely state that refinement slots unlock as **5 at FC5, 6 with War Academy, 7 at FC8, 8 at FC10**. That table does not describe what a Fire Crystal Age account actually sees. An FC3 account on a Gen 2 state has a working Lab and has already reached the 50,000-resource cost tier, which the published ladder places at slot six.

Either the slot table is stale, or slot count is driven by something other than state FC level. Until this is settled, **read your own Lab panel** rather than planning against the table below. Open the Lab at the start of a day, before refining, and count what you actually have.
:::

## Normal Refinement

Normal refinement converts **Meat, Wood, Coal, and Iron** into Fire Crystals. Each refine costs an equal amount of all four resources, and the cost climbs with every refine you make that day.

### The cost ladder

| Refine # | Cost (each of Meat / Wood / Coal / Iron) | Total resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 5,000 | 20,000 |
| 2nd | 10,000 | 40,000 |
| 3rd | 20,000 | 80,000 |
| 4th | 30,000 | 120,000 |
| 5th | 40,000 | 160,000 |
| 6th–8th | 50,000 | 200,000 |

The ladder resets daily.

### What you get back

Each refine returns **1 to 5 Fire Crystals**, weighted heavily toward the low end:

| Fire Crystals | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 40% |
| 2 | 30% |
| 3 | 15% |
| 4 | 10% |
| 5 | 5% |

**Expected value: 2.1 Fire Crystals per refine**, regardless of which slot you are on.

(1 × 0.40) + (2 × 0.30) + (3 × 0.15) + (4 × 0.10) + (5 × 0.05) = **2.10**

That last point is the whole page. **The payout does not scale with the cost.** The sixth refine costs ten times what the first one did and returns exactly the same 2.1 crystals on average.

### The efficiency cliff

| Refine # | Total resources | Resources per expected Fire Crystal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 20,000 | **~9,500** |
| 2nd | 40,000 | ~19,000 |
| 3rd | 80,000 | ~38,100 |
| 4th | 120,000 | ~57,100 |
| 5th | 160,000 | ~76,200 |
| 6th–8th | 200,000 | **~95,200** |

Running a full day of eight refines costs roughly **900,000 total resources** for about **17 Fire Crystals**. Running only the first three costs **140,000** for about **6.3**.

**You get 37% of the crystals for 16% of the resources.**

## The Daily Decision

This is not a "do it all or skip it" building. It is a sliding scale, and where you stop should change day to day.

**Always do slots 1–3.** At under 40,000 resources per Fire Crystal they are close to free. Skipping them is pure loss. This should be as automatic as claiming your Lighthouse rewards.

**Do slots 4–5 on ordinary days.** Around 57,000–76,000 per crystal is still reasonable when your resource income is healthy and no large upgrade is queued.

**Do slots 6–8 only when resources are genuinely surplus.** At ~95,000 per crystal you are paying a real price. Justified when your warehouses are near cap and nothing else needs the resources, or when you are close to a Fire Crystal threshold and want to close the gap now.

**Stop entirely when a building upgrade is imminent.** FC-era construction eats resources in the millions. Burning 900,000 resources on 17 Fire Crystals the day before you needed those resources for a Furnace tier is a bad trade in both directions.

::: tip Track it from the Overview panel
Since the August 2026 update, the **Overview → Daily** tab shows remaining Crystal Laboratory attempts alongside Arena, Treks, the Labyrinth, and Expert Skills. Check it there instead of opening the building — it makes the Lab part of a daily sweep rather than something you remember or forget.
:::

## Super Refinement

Super Refinement is a different operation with a different currency. Instead of resources into Fire Crystals, it converts **Fire Crystals into Refined Fire Crystals** — the material that gates every FC6 and higher upgrade.

**It unlocks at FC6.** Below that, this section is preparation, not practice.

**The structure:**

- **100 Super Refinements per week.**
- Those 100 are split into **five tiers of 20**. Each tier costs more Fire Crystals per Refined Fire Crystal than the tier before it.
- **Once per day, the first Super Refinement of that day is half price**, at whatever tier you currently occupy.

### The design punishes rushing

Read those three rules together and the intent is clear. Someone who blitzes all 100 refinements in two days spends most of them in the expensive upper tiers and collects two half-price discounts. Someone who spreads the same 100 across seven days collects seven discounts and spends longer in the cheap tiers.

**The 100/week cap is a ceiling, not a target.** Treating it as a quota you must fill is how F2P accounts convert a large Fire Crystal reserve into a small Refined Fire Crystal pile at the worst possible rate.

**The F2P pattern:** refine a steady daily number, always taking the half-price first refine, and let the tier climb slowly. If a specific upgrade needs a burst, take the burst — but understand you are paying a premium for the speed.

## Strategy by Player Type

**Free-to-play.** Slots 1–3 daily without exception. Slots 4–5 when income allows. Treat 6–8 as a rare surplus dump. On Super Refinement, take the daily half-price refine and little else — the discount is the entire value proposition.

**Minimal spend (VIP only).** Same as F2P. Nothing in the Lab is purchasable, which makes it one of the few systems where a VIP-only account is on equal footing with a spender. Consistency is the whole edge.

**Heavy spend.** Money buys raw Fire Crystals and resources easily. It **cannot buy past the daily refine cap or the weekly Super Refinement cap.** This is the one bottleneck spending cannot skip, which is why a whale's FC10 timeline is still measured in months.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Skipping the cheap slots.** The first three refines are the best resource conversion in the game. Missing a day is unrecoverable — the ladder resets, it does not accumulate.

2. **Grinding out all eight slots every day on principle.** Completion instinct costs you nearly a million resources for crystals you could have gotten far cheaper by spreading the same effort across more days at lower slots.

3. **Treating the 100 weekly Super Refinements as a quota.** The tier structure makes the last twenty far more expensive than the first twenty. Filling the cap is usually the worst way to spend a Fire Crystal reserve.

4. **Missing the daily half-price Super Refinement.** It is once per day and it does not bank. Over a month that is thirty discounts left on the table.

5. **Planning against the published slot table instead of your own screen.** The widely-cited FC5/War Academy/FC8/FC10 gating does not match observed Fire Crystal Age accounts. Read your Lab.

6. **Refining the day before a large upgrade.** Resources spent here are resources not spent on construction. Check your build queue first.

## Quick Reference

| Action | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Refine slots 1–3 every single day** | ~9,500–38,000 resources per crystal; the best rate available |
| **Stop at 3–5 on ordinary days** | Slot 6 costs ten times slot 1 for the same 2.1 crystals |
| **Save slots 6–8 for full warehouses** | Only worth it when resources have nowhere better to go |
| **Skip entirely before a big upgrade** | FC construction costs dwarf anything the Lab returns |
| **Take the half-price Super Refinement daily** | Once per day, does not bank, and it is the whole edge |
| **Never treat 100/week as a quota** | Tier costs climb; the last twenty are the worst value |
