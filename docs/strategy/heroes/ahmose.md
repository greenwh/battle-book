# Ahmose

::: info Quick take
Gen 4 Infantry. The generation's wall: invulnerability windows, team-wide damage reduction, and a garrison widget. He is genuinely strong where he belongs and does very little where he does not.

**He is not a rally leader and not a Bear hero.** If you are free to play and can only develop one Gen 4 hero, it should be [Lynn](/strategy/heroes/lynn), not Ahmose.
:::

## Acquisition

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| King of Icefield | Shop and ranking rewards |
| State of Power (SvS) | Shop |
| Daily Deals | Irregular |
| Foundry Shop | Requires Foundry Battle participation |

No Lucky Wheel route. That is the whole problem for free players — every channel here rewards either ranking or spending, and none of them produce shards passively the way the Wheel does. A minimal-spend account can accumulate Ahmose slowly, but not on a schedule.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Infantry Attack +370.30%, Infantry Defense +370.30%** — the Gen 4 tier, up from roughly +290% at Gen 3.

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Viper Formation** | His Infantry pauses the attack once every four times, cutting damage taken by Lancers and Marksmen by 30% and by Infantry by 70% for 2 turns |
| **Prayer of Flame** | Friendly Infantry deal +100% damage |
| **Blade of Light** | Friendly Infantry deal +60% extra damage per attack, and the target takes +25% more damage for 1 turn |

Viper Formation is the signature. The trade — skipping one attack in four in exchange for a heavy damage-reduction window on the whole force — is what makes him a defensive anchor rather than a damage piece.

## Exploration skills

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Cthugha's Protection** | Enters an invulnerable state (cannot move or cast, immune to control) and reduces damage taken by 70% for nearby friendly troops for 2s |
| **Daybreak Knife** | Attack × 98% damage to front-line enemies; they take 20% more damage for 2s |
| **Ancestral Blessing** | After casting Cthugha's Protection, recovers Attack × 42% Health over 5s |

## Widget — Guardian's Relic

Expedition Lethality +92.50%, Expedition HP +92.50%.

| Skill | Effect at max |
| --- | --- |
| **Unyielding Determination** | +42% Attack for friendly troops under Cthugha's Protection, 2.5s |
| **Oath of Guardian** | **Defender Troops' Health +15%** |

Oath of Guardian is the reason to own him. A flat 15% Health on defending troops applies to your city and to every garrison you hold, with no proc and no condition. See [Garrison Captain](/combat/garrison-captain).

## Where he belongs

| Role | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Garrison captain / city defense | **Best use.** The widget skill alone justifies him |
| Arena front line | Strong — the invulnerability window buys time for your damage dealers |
| Sunfire Castle and Foundry defense | Strong |
| Attack rally leading | Poor. No offensive rally skill |
| Bear Trap | Poor. Not a Bear hero |

## Skill priority

Expedition, if you are developing him for defense: **Viper Formation first** — it is the mitigation that defines him and it is flat rather than chance-based. Then Blade of Light, then Prayer of Flame.

Exploration, if he is an Arena piece: **Cthugha's Protection first**, then Ancestral Blessing to make the window self-sustaining, then Daybreak Knife.

## F2P verdict

**Skip unless you are already invested.** He has no free acquisition channel, he does nothing on offense, and a well-developed [Logan](/strategy/heroes/logan) covers most of the same defensive ground on an account that already owns him. A lightly built Ahmose loses to a 4-star Logan in every role they share.

If you do spend, he becomes the correct Infantry answer for garrison work and gets stronger again at Gen 5 when paired with Hector.

::: warning Do not bench a developed Gen 3 hero for a fresh Gen 4 one
A 5-star Logan beats a 2-star Ahmose everywhere. The generation buff is real, but star level and skill level dominate it at low investment. Transition when the new hero is actually built, not when it is unlocked.
:::
