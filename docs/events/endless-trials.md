# Endless Trials

<Frequency cadence="Daily" />

::: info Quick take
A permanent daily solo mode. You attack **Wulfgar, the Windhowler** in the Wilderness and try to land the highest damage in a **single attack**. Up to 30 attacks per day, no rallies.

The strategic core is narrower than it looks: **most of your account does not apply here.** City combat buffs, battle items, and Academy research bonuses are all switched off, and troop composition is chosen for you. What is left is your heroes, their skills, their widgets, and their gear. That makes Endless Trials a clean hero-lineup test bench, and a rare place where the answer is found by experiment rather than by reading a tier list.
:::

## How it works

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Boss** | Wulfgar, the Windhowler — a barbarian chieftain from the far north |
| **Where** | The Wilderness |
| **Opens** | Daily, after 00:30 UTC |
| **Attacks** | Up to 30 solo attacks per day |
| **Rallies** | Not permitted |
| **Scored on** | Your **highest single attack** that day, not the total |
| **Rewards** | Daily participation, daily state leaderboard, damage milestones, first-time milestone rewards |
| **Arrived with** | The Fire Crystal Age update, August 2026 |

Because ranking is by best single attack rather than cumulative damage, a run of poor attempts costs you nothing but attempts. There is no downside to experimenting.

## What applies and what does not

This is the part worth reading twice.

| Does **not** apply | **Does** apply |
| --- | --- |
| City combat buffs | Hero skills, stars, and skill levels |
| Battle items | Hero gear, Mastery, and widgets |
| Academy research combat bonuses | Active **pet** perks |
| | **State Position** bonuses |
| | **President skills**, including Frostdragon Tyrant |

**Troop composition is automatic.** The game selects your highest available troop tiers and applies a fixed ratio. You cannot change the Infantry / Lancer / Marksman split, so troop-ratio planning has no role here.

::: tip Do not stockpile battle items for this
A common instinct is to save buffs for a record attempt. City combat buffs and battle items do nothing in Endless Trials. Spend them where they work.

What **is** worth timing: make sure your pet perks are active and check whether a President skill is running before you make a serious attempt.
:::

## Finding your best lineup

Because the usual levers are disabled, the only real variable is which heroes you send. And the obvious answer — your newest, highest-generation heroes — is frequently wrong.

**A well-invested older hero often beats a fresh current-generation one.** Widget level, exclusive gear, star count, and skill levels all carry more weight here than the generation buff does, because there are no external multipliers to dilute them.

**The method, in order:**

1. **Start with your strongest, best-invested team.** Record the number.
2. **Swap one hero at a time.** Changing the whole lineup at once tells you nothing about which hero moved the number.
3. **Repeat each configuration several times before judging it.** Damage varies between attacks. One low result is not evidence.
4. **Keep a written record** of the best number per configuration. Thirty attacks a day is plenty for structured testing, but only if you are not re-deriving yesterday's results.
5. **Re-test after any real upgrade** — a star, a widget level, a Mastery threshold, a new exclusive gear piece. Each of those is a fresh chance at a milestone.

Heroes with high offensive skill multipliers tend to test well. Heroes whose value is conditional — garrison widgets, defender buffs, rally-only skills — tend not to, regardless of how good they are elsewhere.

## Rewards and the daily routine

Four reward streams: daily participation, damage milestones, state leaderboard ranking, and one-time first-clear rewards at new damage thresholds.

**The first-time milestone rewards are the reason to care.** They pay in Mithril, Mythic Gear materials, Charms, Books of Knowledge, and Expert Sigils — exactly the materials that are scarce everywhere else in the game, and several of which are also high-value SvS Preparation items.

**A sensible daily routine takes two minutes:**

1. Open Endless Trials after reset.
2. Confirm pet perks are active.
3. Make a few attacks with your best-known lineup — enough for the daily rewards.
4. Claim any milestone or ranking rewards.
5. Bank the remaining attempts. Use them for structured testing after upgrades, not for grinding.

You do not need all 30 attacks most days.

## Strategy by player type

**Free to play.** This is one of the better free reward streams in the game, because it costs nothing but time and pays in materials you cannot easily farm. Chase personal milestones and ignore the leaderboard.

**Minimal spend.** The same. The leaderboard rewards are real but the top of any state's board is whale territory, and the first-time milestones are where the value is concentrated for everyone else.

**Heavy spend.** Leaderboard placement becomes contestable. Even then the lineup testing above is what moves the number — there is no way to buy past a badly chosen team.

## Common mistakes

1. **Saving battle items and city buffs for a record attempt.** They do not apply. This is the most common misunderstanding about the mode.
2. **Judging a lineup on one attack.** Damage varies. Test each setup several times.
3. **Assuming the newest heroes are best.** Investment beats generation here more than almost anywhere else in the game.
4. **Changing several heroes at once.** You learn nothing about which change mattered.
5. **Burning all 30 attacks daily on the same known lineup.** The daily rewards do not require it. Bank the attempts for post-upgrade testing.
6. **Chasing the leaderboard instead of milestones.** The one-time damage rewards are worth more to most accounts than a ranking position.
7. **Forgetting to claim.** Milestone and ranking rewards sit in the event panel until collected.
