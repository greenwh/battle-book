# Reina

::: info Quick take
Gen 4 Lancer and the most offense-focused hero of the three. Her kit is extra attacks, dodge, and a rally lethality widget — she raises the damage ceiling of a Lancer-led march rather than supporting one.

**Hall of Heroes acquisition.** That means Marks of Valor, which means gems or packs. She is the generation's paid Lancer, the same way Logan was the paid Infantry at Gen 3.
:::

## Acquisition

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Hall of Heroes | Primary. Marks of Valor, bought with gems or from packs |
| Foundry Shop | Secondary, slow |

There is no free channel with meaningful throughput. Hall of Heroes accumulation is possible on a minimal-spend account but takes many months per star.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Lancer Attack +370.30%, Lancer Defense +370.30%**.

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Assassin's Instinct** | +30% normal attack damage for **all** troops |
| **Swift Jive** | All troops gain a 20% chance to dodge normal attacks |
| **Shadow Blade** | Her Lancers have a 25% chance of an extra attack dealing 200% damage |

Assassin's Instinct is the joiner-relevant one — it buffs all troops, not just Lancers, so it carries value when she is the first hero in a rally-joining lineup. See [Rally Joining](/combat/rally-joining) for why only the first hero's first Expedition skill counts for joiners.

Shadow Blade is the rally-leading one, and it is chance-based rather than flat, which makes it swingier than the flat buffs on older rally leaders.

## Exploration skills

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Phantom Assault** | Attack × 420% area damage from behind |
| **Vanishing Technique** | 25% chance to dodge a Normal Attack via Illusion |
| **Poison of Demon** | Inflicts Illusion (hero first), Attack × 140% damage, immobilizes 1.5s |

Poison of Demon targeting heroes first makes her a genuine Arena threat rather than just a damage number.

## Widget — Ninjaken, Raikiri

Expedition Lethality +92.50%, Expedition HP +92.50%.

| Skill | Effect at max |
| --- | --- |
| **Silhouette Strike** | 40% chance of an extra kunai for Attack × 45% on Normal Attack |
| **Fiery Invasion** | **Rally Troops' Lethality +15%** |

Fiery Invasion is a rally-leader skill, not a joiner skill. It is worth having only if you actually lead Lancer rallies.

## Where she belongs

| Role | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Lancer rally leading | **Best use**, if you are the alliance's Lancer lead |
| Castle siege damage | Strong |
| Arena | Strong, especially with Poison of Demon |
| Rally joining | Decent via Assassin's Instinct, but not best in class |
| Garrison | Poor. Nothing defensive in the kit |

## Skill priority

**Only invest Expedition manuals if she is genuinely part of your rally plan.** Mythic Expedition Skill Manuals are scarce.

If she is your rally lead: Shadow Blade first, Assassin's Instinct second, Swift Jive last.
If she is a joiner: Assassin's Instinct only, and stop.

## F2P verdict

**Skip, or take to 4 stars only if you lead rallies.** For a free account the correct Gen 4 target is [Lynn](/strategy/heroes/lynn), who comes off the Lucky Wheel.

For a light spender who is the alliance's rally leader, the picture inverts — Reina is the one hero of the generation whose absence is felt by the whole alliance rather than just your own account. A developed [Mia](/strategy/heroes/mia) still outperforms a low-star Reina, so do not switch until the stars are there.
