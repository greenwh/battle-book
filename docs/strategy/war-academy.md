# War Academy and Fire Crystal Shards

::: info Quick take
The War Academy is a state-gated military research building and the only route to **T11 Helios troops**. Its currency is the **Fire Crystal Shard**, which is slow to earn and expensive to convert.

The thing most guides miss: **a Fire Crystal Shard spent on War Academy research is worth 1,000 SvS Preparation points.** That makes shard timing a bigger decision than shard allocation. Bank shards between events and spend them inside a Prep window, not the moment they arrive.
:::

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Unlocks** | State progression, roughly day 220 on the old timeline — shortly after Gen 4 heroes |
| **Gated by** | The state, not your Furnace. You cannot rush it |
| **Currency** | Fire Crystal Shards, plus large amounts of Steel |
| **Research trees** | Three, one per troop class |
| **Endpoint** | T11 Helios Infantry, Lancer, and Marksman — each unlocked separately |
| **Final node needs** | War Academy at FC5 |

The War Academy is not the Research Center. It does nothing for your economy. Every node in it is troop or combat advancement.

## Where shards come from

| Source | Rate | Daily cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Steel exchange** | 50,000 Steel to 1 Shard | 20 exchanges per day |
| **Fire Crystal exchange** | 10 Fire Crystals to 13 Shards | ~200 Fire Crystals per day |
| **Packs** | Varies | Purchase only |
| **Events and exploration** | Small, irregular | — |

::: warning Two source conflicts, stated honestly
Community sources disagree on the Steel rate. Most guides give **50,000 Steel per Shard**; the community wiki lists 5,000. The higher figure matches more reports and is used throughout this page — check your own exchange screen before planning around either number.

The Fire Crystal exchange cap is also reported inconsistently, as either 200 Fire Crystals or 200 Shards per day. Read your own screen.
:::

::: danger Do not convert Fire Crystals into Shards until the building is finished
Fire Crystals upgrade the War Academy building itself. Shards only buy research inside it. Converting Fire Crystals early starves the building of the levels that gate the research you are buying shards for, and the final Helios node will not open until the building reaches FC5.

**Building first. Shards second.** This is the single most expensive mistake available in this system.
:::

## What T11 actually costs

One complete Helios branch:

| | Per branch | All three branches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Crystal Shards | 13,421 | 40,263 |
| Steel | 3,974,000 | 11,922,000 |
| Base research time | ~271 days | ~813 days |

The final Helios node alone accounts for **2,236 Shards and more than 91 days** of that base timer. With roughly 70% research speed bonuses, one branch comes down to around 159 days of real time.

**Run the numbers on the pure Steel route.** At the cap of 20 Shards per day, one branch is 13,421 ÷ 20 = **671 days**. Steel exchange alone will not get a free player to T11 in any reasonable horizon. The Fire Crystal route is far faster but competes directly with Furnace upgrades and Super Refinement.

The honest conclusion: **for a minimal-spend account, T11 is a multi-year project.** Treat the SvS points below as the near-term return and the troops as the long-term one.

## The SvS overlay — the real reason to care

Fire Crystal Shards spent on War Academy research score **1,000 Preparation points each**. They are high-value on **Day 2 (Research Day)** and also score on **Day 1** and **Day 5**.

This changes the whole system's economics. A shard spent on a random Tuesday buys research. The same shard spent during a Prep window buys the same research **and** 1,000 points.

**The arithmetic.** At 20 Shards per day from Steel exchange alone:

| Banking period | Shards | SvS points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 week | 140 | 140,000 |
| 3 weeks | 420 | 420,000 |
| 5 weeks | 700 | 700,000 |

That is a serious personal score from a currency you were accumulating anyway.

::: tip The rule
Never spend Fire Crystal Shards outside an SvS Preparation window unless completing a node unblocks something you need right now. The research lands whenever. The points only land on Days 1, 2, and 5.
:::

This mirrors the discipline that already applies to Fire Crystals, Expert Sigils, Mithril, and Wild Marks. Shards are simply the newest entry on that list, and one of the most point-dense.

## Order of operations

1. **Finish the building.** Push the War Academy up its Fire Crystal levels toward FC5 before diverting Fire Crystals into Shards. Early nodes open at FC1 and rise through FC2, FC3, and FC4, so there is useful research available while you climb.
2. **Start the Steel exchange immediately, every day.** Twenty exchanges is a two-minute habit and the only free shard income you have. Keep a Steel reserve — research itself consumes millions of Steel on top of Shards.
3. **Pick one branch and finish it.** Each troop class is a separate 13,421-shard commitment. Spreading across all three means three unfinished branches and no T11 at all. Most players take **Infantry first** — it is the troop type that absorbs damage, so the tier upgrade is felt immediately in garrison and rally survival.
4. **Spend inside Prep windows.** See above.

## Strategy by player type

**Free to play.** Steel exchange daily, no Fire Crystal conversion until the building is done, one branch, and every shard spent on an SvS Day 2. Accept that T11 is distant. The points are the payoff you actually collect.

**Minimal spend.** Same discipline. If you buy anything here, buy shards rather than research speedups — speedups are replaceable, and shards are the binding constraint.

**Heavy spend.** Shard packs collapse the timeline substantially. Even then, finish one branch before opening the next, and still spend inside Prep windows.

## Common mistakes

1. **Converting Fire Crystals to Shards before the building is done.** Starves the building that gates the research. The most expensive error in the system.
2. **Spending shards the day you earn them.** Costs 1,000 SvS points per shard for no gain.
3. **Spreading research across all three branches.** Three half-finished trees produce zero T11 troops.
4. **Forgetting the Steel.** Nearly four million Steel per branch, on top of shards. Running the exchange to the cap every day while ignoring the research reserve leaves you shard-rich and Steel-poor.
5. **Treating the War Academy as a Furnace prerequisite.** It is not. It is a separate building with its own Fire Crystal levels.
6. **Assuming T11 is the end.** Older states have moved on to T12 Exalted troops. Helios remains a prerequisite for that path, but it is no longer the ceiling everywhere.
