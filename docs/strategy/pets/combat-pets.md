# Combat Pets

::: info Quick take
Eight combat pets provide 2-hour active skill buffs. Cave Lion (+10% Attack) and Saber-Tooth Tiger (+10% Lethality) are the two best and should lead your leveling order. Two things most guides get wrong: pets unlock through a **leveling chain**, not purely by server age, and **Strengthening Serums cap your realistic progress around level 50** long before Pet Food does. Plan around the serum, not the food.
:::

## The Unlock Chain

Pets are gated **twice**, and confusing the two gates is the most common planning error in this section.

1. **Server generation day.** Each pet generation becomes available to the state at a fixed server age. This is what the [Server Timeline](/reference/server-timeline) tables show.
2. **The pet leveling chain.** Individual pets unlock by leveling the pet before them. This is the gate you actually feel.

The second gate is the real one. A pet can appear in your Beast Cage as available to unlock **ahead of its listed generation day**, provided you have leveled its predecessor far enough. Treat the timeline days as "available by," not "not before."

**Worked example, Gen 2 state at Day 200:**

- Unlocked: Cave Hyena, Musk Ox, Arctic Wolf, Titan Roc, Giant Tapir, Snow Leopard, Giant Elk, Cave Lion, Snow Ape, **Iron Rhino**
- Iron Rhino is listed at Day 280 on the generation timeline but unlocked at Day 200 through the chain behind Snow Ape
- Saber-Tooth Tiger sits further along the same chain and is not yet reached
- Frost Gorilla and Mammoth are still a generation out

**Combat pets on that account: 5 of 8.** Cave Lion, Titan Roc, Snow Leopard, Snow Ape, Iron Rhino.

**Musk Ox is the early gate pet.** Reaching Musk Ox Lv. 15 unlocks Giant Tapir, which provides a passive Pet Food engine. Every later chain step is paid for out of that engine, so Musk Ox is the highest-priority utility pet by a wide margin.

## Leveling Priority

Level in tier order, but read the serum ceiling below before committing to "max" on anything.

1. **Cave Lion** first. +10% Attack is the single strongest pet buff and it applies in nearly every mode.
2. **Saber-Tooth Tiger** second, once unlocked. +10% Lethality pairs with Cave Lion for the strongest offensive combination available.
3. **Frost Gorilla** third. +10% Health is the best survivability buff.
4. **Mammoth** and **Snow Ape** in parallel. Defense and deployment capacity — both A-tier and situational.
5. **Iron Rhino** only if you lead rallies. +150,000 Rally Capacity does nothing for a joiner.
6. **Titan Roc** is useful but carries a Bear Trap caveat (below).
7. **Snow Leopard — park it.** B-tier, and march speed is the least valuable thing on this list. Unlock it for the chain and leave it there.

::: danger The Strengthening Serum ceiling
**Strengthening Serums are the hardest bottleneck in the pet system** — harder than Pet Food, Taming Manuals, or Energizing Potions. They gate the upper level bands, and income is slow enough that a serum shortage will stall you regardless of how much food you have banked.

**Do not push any pet past level 50 until your serum reserves are comfortable.**

The practical consequence: "level Cave Lion to max" is the right *direction* but the wrong *instruction*. Take Cave Lion to 50, then take the next pet to 50, and keep going wide rather than deep. Breadth at level 50 across four pets beats one pet at 70 and three at 20, because each pet's active skill is a separate buff you can stack — and because the serum spend to go deep is disproportionate.
:::

## Refinement Strategy

Pet refinement adds passive **Troop Lethality and Troop Health** using Wild Marks. It is RNG — you spend marks and take the roll. Refinement materials are **not recoverable**.

**The rule is gated by refinement stage, not by which pet.** This is the part most guides state incorrectly.

| Refinement stage | Mark to use | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| **Gray → Blue** | Common Wild Marks | Every pet, no exceptions |
| **Blue → Purple** | Common Wild Marks | Only pets with a **max-100 ceiling** (Cave Lion, Snow Ape) |
| **Purple → Gold** | **Advanced Wild Marks** | Reserved exclusively for this stage |

**Advanced Wild Marks have exactly one correct use: purple-to-gold.** Spending them on an earlier stage is not "wasteful on a bad pet" — it is wasteful *at all*, on any pet, because Commons do the same job at those stages. The error people make is thinking the rule sorts by pet quality. It sorts by stage.

**Refinement order:** Cave Lion first, Snow Ape second. Snow Leopard stays parked here too — do not spend marks of any type on it.

## Titan Roc Bear Trap Warning

Titan Roc's Armor Rift (-5% Enemy HP) is useful in PvP but can hurt you in Bear Trap.

**If your alliance reliably kills the bear with time remaining → do NOT use Armor Rift.** It lowers the bear's maximum HP, which reduces your alliance's total damage score, which reduces everyone's rewards.

**If your alliance does NOT kill the bear in time → Armor Rift is safe.** Reducing the bear's HP helps you get closer to a kill, and you were not going to max the damage score anyway.

This is a per-alliance decision. Strong alliances should disable Titan Roc for Bear Trap. Developing alliances can use it freely.

## Activation Routine

Combat pet skills last **2 hours** with roughly a **20-hour cooldown**. Time them for the event window — activating three hours early wastes the buff entirely.

**Standard offensive activation (most events):**
1. Cave Lion (Feral Anthem)
2. Saber-Tooth Tiger (Apex Assault)
3. Snow Ape (Tumbling Power) — for rally events

**Standard defensive activation (garrison duty, defending):**
1. Frost Gorilla (Earthbound Vigor)
2. Mammoth (Hardened Skin)

**Rally leader activation:**
1. Cave Lion + Saber-Tooth Tiger + Snow Ape (offensive)
2. Iron Rhino (Rallying Beast) — increased rally capacity

::: warning Alliance Championship changed in August 2026
**Pet skills now activate automatically when squads are deployed in Alliance Championship**, with bonuses calculated from your pet skill *levels* at the moment of deployment. Manual pre-activation is no longer required and the old registration-snapshot trap no longer applies to pets.

This does not change anything for other events, and it does not obviously extend to gem buffs or other timed bonuses — only pets were named in the patch notes. Treat everything else as still snapshot-based until tested. See [Pet Buffs](/combat/pet-buffs).
:::

Pet skills also **auto-activate in the Labyrinth**, in Cave of Monsters and Gaia Heart zones.

See [Pre-Battle Checklist](/combat/pre-battle-checklist) for the complete pre-event sequence including gem buffs and troop ratios.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Spending Advanced Wild Marks below purple-to-gold.** Commons handle every earlier stage on every pet. Advanced marks have one job.

2. **Pushing one pet deep instead of several to 50.** Strengthening Serums make depth expensive. Active skills stack, so breadth buys more.

3. **Reading generation days as hard gates.** Pets unlock through a leveling chain and routinely become available ahead of their listed day. Check the Beast Cage, not the calendar.

4. **Leveling Snow Leopard.** B-tier, march speed, no competitive application worth the materials. Unlock and park.

5. **Activating pet skills at the wrong time.** Two-hour duration. Activation three hours before an event is a wasted cooldown.

6. **Using Titan Roc Armor Rift in Bear Trap when your alliance kills the bear.** One player's mistake, alliance-wide reward reduction.

7. **Still manually pre-activating pets for Alliance Championship registration.** As of August 2026 this is automatic at deployment. The habit is harmless but the cooldown it burns is not.
