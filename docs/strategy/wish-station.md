# Wish Station

::: info Quick take
The Wish Station replaces your old Mailbox once your city enters the Fire Crystal Age. Completing Wishes pays **Wish Sigils**, which buy materials in the **Wish Shop** for a set of nine new entertainment buildings. It arrived in the August 17, 2026 update and it is new enough that reward rates and shop contents are not yet documented anywhere. What is known is the shape of the loop and the fact that it is entirely free — no pack purchase is required to complete Wishes or build the facilities.
:::

## What It Is

The Fire Crystal Age is not only a new upgrade currency. It is also a narrative and city-development layer: survivors stop merely surviving and start wanting things. Survivor Profiles complete, giving each survivor an identity, and the **Mailbox becomes the Wish Station** — the place survivors post what they want next.

Mechanically it is a progression track sitting on top of Furnace level.

## The Loop

1. **Wishes unlock progressively.** New Wishes become available as your Furnace level rises and as prerequisite Wishes are completed. You do not get the whole board at once.
2. **Completing a Wish pays Wish Sigils.**
3. **Wish Sigils are spent in the Wish Shop** for exclusive construction materials.
4. **Those materials build the nine entertainment facilities** that come with the Fire Crystal Age.

Century Games has stated the construction materials are obtainable through ordinary daily play, with no payment requirement.

## When It Unlocks

::: warning The announced gate and the observed gate do not match
**Announced:** the Wish Station appears once the Furnace reaches **Fire Crystal Lv. 1** — that is, on entering the Fire Crystal Age.

**Observed on a Gen 2 state, August 2026:** an account at **FC3 has the Wish Station**. A second account on the same state at **FC2 does not**.

Two readings fit. Either the announced FC1 figure is imprecise, or the *building* exists from FC1 while the Wish board itself only surfaces once a prerequisite chain has been cleared — which the official wording allows, since it ties Wish availability to Furnace level and prerequisite completion rather than to a single unlock event.

Plan around **FC3** if you want a number you have seen work. Check at every FC tier if you are below that.
:::

## The Chief Order Reshuffle

The Wish Station does not arrive alone. Entering the Fire Crystal Age also changes your **Chief Orders**.

- **Some older Chief Orders are removed** on entry.
- **Three new ones are added:** **Crunch Time** (resource production), **Advanced Training** (soldier training), and **Dawn Alliance Commendation** (city development).

If your Chief Order rotation suddenly looks different after an FC tier, this is why. It is not a bug and nothing was taken away without replacement — the set is being retargeted at Fire Crystal Age priorities.

## What Is Not Known Yet

This section exists so nobody plans against numbers that do not exist. As of late August 2026, no source publishes:

- **Wish Sigil income per Wish**, or how it scales with Furnace level
- **Wish Shop contents and prices**
- **What the nine entertainment buildings actually do** — whether they carry Power, provide stat or economy bonuses, or are primarily cosmetic

That last one is the important gap. Until the buildings' effects are known, there is no way to say whether Wish Sigils are worth prioritizing over any other daily activity.

::: tip What to do in the meantime
Complete Wishes as they appear — they cost nothing but attention and the Sigils bank. **Do not spend Sigils in the Wish Shop until you know what the buildings do.** An unspent currency keeps its options; a spent one does not. This is the same discipline that applies to Advanced Wild Marks and generic Mythic hero shards.
:::

## Strategy by Player Type

**Free-to-play and minimal spend.** Bank Sigils, complete Wishes opportunistically, hold shop purchases. Nothing here is time-limited in a way that punishes waiting, and the information gap is temporary.

**Heavy spend.** Same answer, for the same reason. There is no shortcut available and nothing to buy your way past — the constraint is Furnace level and Wish prerequisites, not currency.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Assuming it unlocks at FC1 and worrying when it doesn't appear.** Observation says FC3 on at least one Gen 2 state. If you are at FC1 or FC2 without a Wish Station, that is normal.

2. **Spending Wish Sigils before the buildings are understood.** Unspent currency is optionality. Nobody yet knows which materials matter.

3. **Panicking about missing Chief Orders.** The FC Age retargets the set. The removals are intentional and three new orders replace them.

4. **Treating this as a power system.** It may turn out to be one. As of now nobody has published evidence either way, and the Battle Book will not guess.
