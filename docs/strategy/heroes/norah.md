# Norah

::: info Quick take
Gen 5 Lancer, reached through **King of Icefield** and the shops around it. Spelled **Norah**, not Nora — older community charts use the short form.

She is the odd one of the generation. Her strongest Expedition skill, **Combined Arms**, buffs Infantry and Marksmen — not Lancers. That makes her a formation support piece rather than a Lancer carry, and it means her value depends on what the rest of your march looks like rather than on how good your Lancers are.
:::

This is the Hall of Chiefs channel in its mature form — see the note on the [Hero Roster](/strategy/heroes/roster) about why that channel changes name after a state's first SvS. No Lucky Wheel route, so there is no passive gem-saving path to her.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Lancer Attack +444.36%, Lancer Defense +444.36%**.

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Combined Arms** | **Infantry and Marksmen** take 15% less damage and deal 15% more |
| **Sneak Strike** | Lancers have a 20% chance of 100% extra damage on attack |
| **Momentum** | Every 5 Lancer attacks, **all troops** get +25% Damage Dealt and −25% Damage Taken for 2 turns |

**Combined Arms is the reason to field her and the reason she is hard to place.** A flat 15% both ways on two of the three troop types is a large, unconditional effect — but it does nothing for the troop type she commands. She is at her best in a mixed march where Lancers are a minority, which is the opposite of how most Lancer heroes are used.

Momentum is the all-troop skill and therefore the one that matters in the joiner slot. Sneak Strike is Lancer-only and chance-based, which makes it the weakest of the three for most accounts.

## Exploration skills

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Barrage** | Five grenades at random targets, heroes first, for **84%** of Attack each as area damage |
| **Flashbang** | **70%** of Attack and a 1.5s stun |
| **Valkyrie Cry** | All troops' Attack **+5%** |

Barrage targeting heroes first makes her a real Arena threat, and Flashbang's stun is the kind of hard control that decides hero fights.

## Where she belongs

| Role | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Mixed-formation support | **Best use.** Combined Arms is the payoff |
| Rally joining | Good, via Momentum |
| Arena | **Strong** — Barrage plus Flashbang is real control |
| Garrison | Good, via True Grit |
| Lancer rally leading | Workable, but she is not built to carry Lancers |

## Skill priority

**Combined Arms to Lv5 first**, and it is not close. Flat, unconditional, and it covers two troop types.

Then Momentum, which is what she contributes as a joiner. Sneak Strike last — Lancer-only and chance-gated.

Exploration, if she is an Arena piece: Flashbang first for the stun, then Barrage, then Valkyrie Cry.

## F2P verdict

**Skip her unless Lancer is a clear hole in your roster — and even then, think about it.**

She has no free acquisition channel, and the usual warning applies with extra force here: a basic Norah unlock is worth less than a developed hero already in your formation. A 4-star [Mia](/strategy/heroes/mia) with maxed skills outperforms a 1-star Norah comfortably.

The case for her is narrow but real. If you run mixed marches and already hold strong Infantry and Marksman heroes, Combined Arms is a buff to the troops you actually field, delivered by the slot you were under-using anyway. That is a genuine argument — it is just not an argument most free accounts are in a position to act on.

**Priority within Gen 5:** third. Hector first, Gwen second if your Marksman needs the upgrade, Norah last.
