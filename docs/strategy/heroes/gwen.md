# Gwen

::: info Quick take
Gen 5 Marksman and the most purely offensive hero of the generation. Highest Attack of the three, a rally lethality widget, and a kit built entirely around raising damage — hers and everyone else's.

**Hall of Heroes acquisition**, which means Marks of Valor, which means gems or packs. She is the generation's paid damage hero.
:::

## Acquisition

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Hall of Heroes** | Primary. Marks of Valor, bought with gems or from packs |
| Foundry Shop | Secondary, slow |

No free channel with meaningful throughput. Accumulation on a minimal-spend account is possible but measured in months per star.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Marksman Attack +444.36%, Marksman Defense +444.36%**.

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Eagle Vision** | Target takes **+25%** more damage |
| **Air Dominance** | After every 5 attacks, all troops deal **+100%** bonus damage, and the target takes **+15%** extra damage from its next attack |
| **Blastmaster** | Marksmen deal **+50%** extra damage on every fourth attack |

**Eagle Vision is the joiner skill and the quietly valuable one.** A flat damage-amplification debuff on the target raises the output of every hero in the march, not just hers. In the first-hero joiner slot, that is close to the best thing a Marksman can contribute — see [Rally Joining](/combat/rally-joining) for why only the first hero's first Expedition skill counts.

Air Dominance is all-troop and periodic; Blastmaster is Marksman-only. Both reward sustained exchanges, which makes her a better fit for long sieges than for quick strikes.

## Exploration skills

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Salvo** | **252%** of Attack as area damage to rear enemies, heroes first, plus −50% Attack Speed for 2s |
| **Sky Sniper** | **140%** of Attack, with a 50% chance to double |
| **Hellfire** | Burns an area for **49%** of Attack per second for 3s |

Salvo hitting the back line first, heroes prioritized, is exactly what you want against the ranged damage dealers that decide Arena fights. The Attack Speed debuff on top makes it a tempo skill as well as a damage one.

## Widget — Wings of Hope

Expedition Lethality +111%, Expedition HP +111%.

| Skill | Effect at max |
| --- | --- |
| **Fire Support Unit** | Every skill cast fires an automated secondary attack at a random target, heroes first, for **70%** of Attack |
| **Marauder** | **Rally Troops' Lethality +15%** |

Marauder is a rally-leader skill, not a joiner skill — worth levelling only if you actually lead Marksman rallies.

## Where she belongs

| Role | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Marksman rally leading | **Best use** |
| Rally joining | **Strong**, via Eagle Vision |
| Arena | **Strong** — Salvo is one of the better back-line answers in the game |
| Sustained sieges | Strong; both periodic skills need time to cycle |
| Garrison | Poor. Nothing defensive in the kit |

## Skill priority

**Eagle Vision to Lv5 first** if she will ever join a rally — it is flat, it is first in the tree, and it multiplies the whole march.

If she is your dedicated Marksman rally lead, Air Dominance second and Blastmaster third. Blastmaster is the most skippable of the three.

Exploration: Salvo first, then Sky Sniper, then Hellfire.

## F2P verdict

**Second priority within Gen 5, behind [Hector](/strategy/heroes/hector) — and only if Marksman is the weak slot in your roster.**

If you have been running [Lynn](/strategy/heroes/lynn) and she is developed, she still does a job Gwen does not: Lynn's Melancholic Ballad is a flat enemy-damage reduction that applies defensively, where Gwen is offense throughout. They are not straight substitutes, and an account that leads rallies with a developed Lynn should not rush to replace her.

For a free account, the honest answer is that Gwen is a want rather than a need. Hector is the generation's free pick; Gwen is the upgrade you take if your Marksman slot is genuinely behind and you are willing to spend on Marks of Valor.
