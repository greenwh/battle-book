# Philly

::: info Quick take
Gen 2 Lancer, and the first readily accessible Mythic **healer** on any state. She does not out-damage her generation-mates — she keeps the rest of the lineup alive long enough for their kits to matter, which is a different and often more valuable job.

Her ceiling is Exploration: Arena and hero combat. Her Expedition kit is useful but not exceptional, and she is replaced as the Lancer rally answer by [Mia](/strategy/heroes/mia) at Gen 3.
:::

## Acquisition

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **King of Icefield** | Primary. Shop and ranking rewards |

King of Icefield runs on a seasonal hero rotation, so her availability comes in windows. She is one of the more forgiving Mythic heroes to accumulate on a minimal-spend account, because KoI pays out on participation as well as ranking.

## Exploration skills — her real value

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **First Aid** | Restores Health equal to Attack × 280% to **all** friendly heroes |
| **Restorative Hands** | Restores Attack × 140% to the **weakest** friendly hero |
| **Paralytic Lotion** | Poisons the target, disabling for 1s and dealing Attack × 196% |

First Aid is the skill that makes her. A team-wide, Attack-scaling heal stays relevant for many generations, because it scales with her stats rather than with a fixed number.

Restorative Hands auto-targeting the lowest-health hero means it rarely wastes, which is unusual among single-target heals.

**Skill priority:** First Aid to Lv5 first. Restorative Hands second — it fills the gaps between First Aid casts. Paralytic Lotion last; scaling it only raises damage, not the interrupt duration.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Attack +240.19%, Defense +240.19%** — the Gen 2 tier.

Her Expedition kit is a damage-reduction proc, a burst-damage proc, and a modest troop buff. Community sources disagree on the exact skill names, which is a sign of how little attention this half of her kit gets.

**The practical read:** she functions as a **joiner first, rally leader only if you have no better Lancer**. Her damage-reduction proc is the most impactful part and benefits every march she is in.

::: warning Do not pour Mythic Expedition Skill Manuals into Philly out of habit
Manuals are scarce and her Expedition skills are useful rather than exceptional. If you are undecided, skip it and save them. You can return to her Expedition tree later; you cannot recover manuals spent on a hero you replace.
:::

## Widget — Pharmacologica

Exploration: Attack +540, Defense +540, Health +5,400. Expedition: Lethality +60%, Health +60%.

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Extraction** | Increases her herbal healing effects by 70% |
| **First Aid Training** | **Defending Troops' Health +15%** |

Extraction multiplies the thing she is already best at. First Aid Training is a garrison skill and is the reason to keep her widget levelled even after Mia takes the Lancer rally slot — see [Garrison Captain](/combat/garrison-captain).

| Lancer rally leading | Only until Mia is built |
| Bear Trap | Poor |

## Long-term outlook

Philly is one of the heroes worth keeping developed past her generation. Heroes with unique mechanics — Jeronimo's passive, Natalia's passive, Mia's all-troop buffs, Philly's team heal — stay useful for ten generations or more. Heroes with only raw stats and standard skills last two to four.

She is the Lancer **you replace in Expedition and keep in Exploration.** Mia takes the rally slot; Philly keeps the Arena slot and the garrison widget.
