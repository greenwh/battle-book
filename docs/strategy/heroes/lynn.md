# Lynn

::: info Quick take
Gen 4 Marksman, and **the Lucky Wheel hero of the generation**. That makes her the free player's target and the only Gen 4 hero worth gem-saving for.

She is a support-damage hybrid: a debuff cleanse, an anti-heal, and an all-troop damage buff. Community consensus is blunt about the free path — **take Lynn, skip the other two.**
:::

## Acquisition

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Lucky Wheel** | Primary. The gem-efficient channel |
| Foundry Shop | Secondary |

The Lucky Wheel principle applies unchanged: **120 spins per event is the value ceiling**, and past that the cost per shard rises sharply. Take her to **4 stars**, which is what unlocks Level 5 skills, then stop and bank gems for the next generation's Wheel hero.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Marksman Attack +370.30%, Marksman Defense +370.30%**.

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Song of Lion** | 40% chance to increase damage dealt by 50% for **all** troops |
| **Melancholic Ballad** | All enemy troops deal **20% less damage** |
| **Oonai Cadenza** | Her Marksmen gain +5% Attack every 3 attacks, stacking, lasting the whole battle |

Melancholic Ballad is the quietly excellent one. A flat 20% reduction in enemy damage output, with no proc and no condition, applies in every fight she is in — offense, defense, garrison. Flat beats chance-based, and this is flat.

Oonai Cadenza rewards long fights. In a short rally it barely stacks; in a sustained exchange it compounds hard.

## Exploration skills

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Hymn of Sidrak** | Clears all debuffs (Freeze, Stun, and so on) for all troops, +7% Attack for 5s, debuff immunity during that window |
| **Lethal Finale** | Attack × 300% penetrating damage along a line |
| **Discordant Tune** | Enemy Attack Speed down 3%, and **enemy healing reduced 60%** |

Discordant Tune's healing reduction is the standout. Against any lineup running a healer — and most Arena lineups do — cutting healing by 60% changes the outcome more than raw damage would.

## Widget — Ella's Tear

Expedition Lethality +92.50%, Expedition HP +92.50%.

| Skill | Effect at max |
| --- | --- |
| **Aira's Elegy** | +15% Attack until end of battle after casting Hymn of Sidrak |
| **Iranon's Determination** | **Defender Troops' Lethality +15%** |

Iranon's Determination pairs directly with [Ahmose](/strategy/heroes/ahmose)'s Defender Health widget. An account that holds both has a genuinely reinforced garrison.

## Where she belongs

| Role | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Rally joining | **Strong.** Song of Lion buffs all troops, so it carries in the joiner slot |
| Marksman rally leading | Good |
| Arena | **Strong**, largely on Discordant Tune |
| Garrison | Good, via the widget |
| Bear Trap | Useful but not a specialist |

## Skill priority

**Melancholic Ballad to Lv5 first.** Flat, unconditional, and it applies in every single engagement.

Then Song of Lion (the all-troop buff is what makes her a good joiner), then Oonai Cadenza.

Exploration, if you run Arena: Discordant Tune first, then Hymn of Sidrak, then Lethal Finale.

## F2P verdict

**Build her.** She is free, she is the first free Marksman of the generation genuinely worth having, and nothing in Gen 5 makes her obsolete — the Gen 5 Marksman, Gwen, is a paid hero who takes the offensive slot rather than Lynn's defensive one.

**The plan:** Lynn to 4 stars, Melancholic Ballad to Lv5, then stop and bank gems for the Gen 5 Wheel.

::: tip Do not bench a developed Greg for a fresh Lynn
The same rule as everywhere else. A 5-star [Greg](/strategy/heroes/greg) with maxed skills beats a 2-star Lynn. Transition when she is built, not when she unlocks.
:::
