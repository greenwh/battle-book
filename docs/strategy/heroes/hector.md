# Hector

::: info Quick take
Gen 5 Infantry, and **the Lucky Wheel hero of the generation**. That makes him the free player's target and the one Gen 5 hero to plan around.

He is more than the usual generational step up. **Rampant** buffs Marksman damage alongside Infantry damage, which is unusual for an Infantry hero and is why he has displaced Flint as the go-to free Infantry rally lead — see [Bear Trap](/events/bear-trap), where a Marksman-heavy formation with an Infantry lead is the standard shape.
:::

## Acquisition

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Lucky Wheel** | Primary. The gem-efficient channel |
| Shops and events offering Infantry shards | Secondary, slow |

The Lucky Wheel principle applies unchanged: **120 spins per event is the value ceiling**, and past that the cost per shard rises sharply. Take him to **4 stars**, which unlocks Level 5 skills, then stop and bank gems for the Gen 6 Wheel.

Because he is a Wheel hero, he is also the generation's most *predictable* project — you can see the event coming and save toward it, which is not true of Norah or Gwen.

## Expedition skills

Base troop buff at max: **Infantry Attack +444.36%, Infantry Defense +444.36%** — the Gen 5 tier, up from roughly +370% at Gen 4.

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Survival Instincts** | 40% chance to cut Damage Taken by **50%** for all troops |
| **Rampant** | Infantry Damage Dealt **+200%** and Marksman Damage Dealt **+100%**. Lasts 10 attacks, each attack's boost at 85% of the previous one |
| **Blitz** | All troops have a 25% chance to deal bonus damage at **+200% Attack** |

**Rampant is the skill that matters, and it is worth understanding properly.** It buffs two troop types, not one. Infantry heroes normally buff Infantry and nothing else, which is why a Marksman-heavy rally has historically wanted a Marksman lead. Hector breaks that: he leads with Infantry survivability and still contributes most of a Marksman buff.

The decay is real, though. At 85% per attack the buff is near full strength for the first three or four attacks and noticeably faded by the tenth. He rewards short, decisive engagements more than long grinds.

## Exploration skills

| Skill | Effect at Lv5 |
| --- | --- |
| **Sword Whirlwind** | Attack Speed **+120%**, plus immunity to Freeze, Stun and other control for 4s |
| **Desperado** | Damage Taken reduced **60%** while below 50% Health |
| **Adrenaline Surge** | Attack **+48%** while below 50% Health |

Desperado and Adrenaline Surge are both low-health triggers, which makes him a genuine Arena bruiser rather than a pure wall — he gets more dangerous as he gets hurt. Sword Whirlwind's control immunity also answers the stun-and-lock lineups that beat slower Infantry heroes.

## Widget — Steel Fangs

Expedition Lethality +111%, Expedition HP +111%.

| Skill | Effect at max |
| --- | --- |
| **Reaper's Embrace** | Extends Sword Whirlwind by 1.5s and heals **15%** of damage dealt |
| **Goliath** | **Defender Troops' Attack +15%** |

Goliath is the garrison skill. Note it pairs with [Ahmose](/strategy/heroes/ahmose)'s Defender Health, [Lynn](/strategy/heroes/lynn)'s Defender Lethality, and [Norah](/strategy/heroes/norah)'s Defender Defense — four widgets covering four different defender stats. An account holding several of them has a genuinely reinforced city.

## Where he belongs

| Role | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Infantry rally leading | **Best use.** The F2P rally lead of the current meta |
| Bear Trap | **Strong** — Rampant's Marksman half is built for that formation |
| Garrison | Good, via Goliath |
| Arena | Strong, on the low-health triggers |
| Rally joining | Survival Instincts is all-troop, so he carries in the joiner slot |

## Skill priority

**Rampant to Lv5 first.** It is the reason to own him and the reason he outranks Flint.

Then Survival Instincts — it is all-troop, which makes it the skill that matters when he is joining rather than leading. Blitz last; the 25% proc makes it the swingiest of the three.

Exploration, if you run Arena: Sword Whirlwind first (the control immunity is the hard part to replace), then Desperado, then Adrenaline Surge.

## F2P verdict

**Build him. He is the best free hero in several generations.**

The case is simple: he is on the Wheel, he replaces Flint in the role most free accounts actually use an Infantry hero for, and his signature skill makes him useful in a formation shape he does not nominally belong to. Gen 4 was a quiet generation for free players — Gen 5 is not.

**The plan:** Hector to 4 stars, Rampant to Lv5, then bank gems for Gen 6.

::: tip Generic Mythic shards are worth holding for a Wheel hero
If you have been banking generic Mythic Hero Shards rather than spending them on a current-generation hero, Hector is a sound place to land them — a Wheel hero you were going to chase anyway, where the shards shorten a project you have already committed to rather than starting a new one.
:::

::: warning Do not bench a developed Flint for a fresh Hector
The usual rule. A 4-star Flint with Burning Resolve and Immolation at Lv5 beats a 1-star Hector with Lv2 skills, generational buff included. Transition when Hector is built, not when he unlocks.
:::
