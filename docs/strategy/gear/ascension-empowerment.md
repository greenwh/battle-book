# Ascension, Empowerment, and Mastery Forging

::: info Quick take
These are not four alternatives. They are **four stages of one ladder**, and each is the prerequisite for the next:

**Enhancement → Mastery Forging → Ascension → Empowerment**

The most common misunderstanding is treating Mastery Forging as an optional side upgrade. It is not optional. You cannot ascend a piece without it, and you cannot empower a piece without ascending it first.
:::

## The ladder

| Stage | What it does | Gate | Currency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Enhancement** | Levels the piece 1 to 100 | None | Enhancement XP, fodder gear |
| **Mastery Forging (MF)** | Multiplies every stat the piece gives | Furnace 20, and the piece at Enhancement 20 | Essence Stones |
| **Ascension** | Converts Mythic gear to **Legendary** gear | State progression, plus Enhancement 100 and a Mastery threshold | Mythic Hero Gear |
| **Empowerment** | Stat bumps and milestone skills on Legendary gear | **Mastery Forging Lv 11**, then Legendary enhancement 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 | Mithril + Mythic Hero Gear |

After ascension, enhancement **resets to 1 and the cap is still 100**. You climb the same 100 levels again, on a much stronger base.

## Enhancement

The bottom rung, and the only one with no gate. Feed the piece Enhancement XP components or surplus gear. Each rarity of fodder is worth a fixed number of points: grey 10, green 30, blue 60, purple 150.

Nothing here is subtle. The judgment is which slots to feed, which is covered on [Hero Gear](/strategy/gear/hero-gear).

## Mastery Forging

Available on gold-quality gear once your Furnace is at 20 and the piece is at Enhancement 20. It applies a percentage multiplier to the piece's Gear Strength and to every other stat the piece provides.

::: warning Mastery Forging does not stop at 5
Older guides — and an earlier version of this site — described Mastery Forging as a 1-to-5 system. It is not. Mastery runs well past 10, and **Level 11 is the gate for Empowerment**. Any advice built on a 1-to-5 ceiling is stale.
:::

**Cost structure.** Each Mastery level is **5 forge steps**. At the Lv10 to Lv11 band, each step costs **22 Essence Stones** and adds +2% Gear Strength and +2% Gear Stats. That is 110 stones for the level, taking Gear Stats from +100% to +110%.

The general formula this implies: **Mastery level N costs 10 × N Essence Stones**, as five steps of 2N each.

| Target level | Essence Stones |
| --- | --- |
| Mastery 5 | 50 |
| Mastery 7 | 70 |
| Mastery 10 | 100 |
| Mastery 11 | 110 |

From Mastery 11 upward, levels also consume **Mythic Hero Gear** on top of stones. The quantity per level is not yet published — record it from your own confirm screen the first time you forge past 10.

**Changed in August 2026.** Once Gear Mastery reaches Level 4 or above, upgrades moved from full-level steps to **sub-stage steps**. The total Essence Stone cost is unchanged; what changed is that partial progress toward the next Mastery level is now visible and bankable instead of invisible.

**The golden rule still holds:** concentrate Mastery on one piece at a time. A single piece deep in Mastery outperforms the same stones spread across three pieces, because the multiplier compounds against that piece's whole stat block.

## Ascension

Ascension converts a **Mythic** piece into a **Legendary** piece, raising both its level ceiling and its stats.

**Requirements:**

- The state must have progressed far enough — this is server-gated, not personal
- The piece at **Enhancement 100**
- The piece at a **Mastery threshold**
- **Mythic Hero Gear** as sacrifice material

::: warning Two open questions, flagged rather than guessed
**The exact Mastery gate for ascension is not confirmed.** Century's own help centre states Enhancement 100 and Mastery 10. The Empowerment gate one stage later is verifiably **Mastery 11** — see below — which makes a Mastery 10 ascension gate plausible but unproven. Read your own confirm screen.

**Whether the sacrificed Mythic pieces must match the troop type** is also unconfirmed. Check the confirm screen before feeding anything you would rather keep.
:::

Reforged Mythic gear **can** be used as ascension and empowerment material. That makes reforging replaced pieces a genuine supply line rather than a dead end.

## Empowerment

Once a piece is Legendary and re-enhanced, Empowerment adds stats and unlocks milestone skills.

::: danger The gate is Mastery Forging Lv 11, not Lv 10
The Empowerment screen states it in red: *Reach Mastery Lv. 11 to unlock empowerment.* The Empower button renders at Mastery 10, but the action is refused.

Most community guides, and Century's own help centre, describe the gate as Mastery 10. **They are wrong, or at least incomplete.** This page follows the in-game screen, read directly in September 2026. If you have parked a piece at Mastery 10 expecting to empower it, you are one full Mastery level short.
:::

**Cost per empowerment: 10 Mithril + 3 Mythic Hero Gear.** Mithril is generally the easy half. Mythic Hero Gear is the constraint — see below.

**What one step buys.** A worked example from a Legendary Infantry piece going from +19 to +20:

| Stat | +19 | +20 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power | 840,700 | **1,056,350** |
| Hero Defense | 1,070 | 1,080 |
| Hero Health | 8,032 | 8,100 |
| Escort Defense | 356 | 360 |
| Escort Health | 2,676 | 2,700 |
| Infantry Health | +119.00% | +120.00% |

That is **+215,650 power on a single piece**, and it also crosses a milestone.

### The milestone ladder alternates Expedition and Exploration

Milestones land at enhancement 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 on the Legendary piece — and they alternate between skills that matter in world-map combat and skills that only matter in hero combat.

| Milestone | Type | Effect (Infantry example) | Combat value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **+20** | Expedition | Infantry Defense +20.0% | **High** |
| +40 | Exploration | Hero Health Up +7.5% | Arena and Exploration only |
| **+60** | Expedition | Infantry Attack +30.0% | **High** |
| +80 | Exploration | Hero Defense Up +15.0% | Arena and Exploration only |

**This is the strategic consequence of the whole page.** After you clear +20 on your priority pieces, the next milestone that changes a rally or a garrison is **+60**, not +40. Do not treat +40 as a goal in its own right. Treat +21 through +59 as an enhancement grind toward +60.

## Mythic Hero Gear is the binding constraint

Count the claims against it:

| Claim | Mythic Hero Gear needed |
| --- | --- |
| One empowerment step | 3 |
| One ascension | 2 or more |
| Each Mastery level past 10 | Unknown, additional |

A player running two priority pieces toward +20 and two more toward ascension needs **ten or more pieces** in hand. Most accounts run this at or near zero.

**Sources:** the Arena Shop (a Mythic chest runs around 12,000 Arena Coins), Hall of Heroes, gear chests, and event rewards.

::: tip When you have to strip a hero for material, strip the Exploration hero before the Arena hero
The Arena Shop is what resupplies this currency. Weakening the hero that farms the coins that buy the gear is self-defeating. Exploration has no comparable feedback loop.
:::

**Never use Mythic Hero Gear as enhancement fodder.** At scarcity it is the most valuable item in the gear economy, and enhancement XP is available from anything.

## SvS timing

The stats land whenever you spend. The points only land on the right Preparation day.

| Material | Prep day | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mithril | Day 5 | Very point-dense — bank it for the window |
| Essence Stones | Day 5 | Gear and hero upgrade day |
| Hero Widgets | Day 5 | |
| Chief Gear and Charm materials | Days 1 and 5 | |

If you are within a couple of weeks of a Prep phase and the upgrade is not urgent, hold. This is the same discipline that applies to Fire Crystal Shards and Expert Sigils.

## Common mistakes

1. **Parking a piece at Mastery 10 expecting to empower it.** The gate is 11.
2. **Chasing the +40 milestone.** It is Exploration-only. Grind through it to +60.
3. **Spreading Mastery across pieces.** The multiplier compounds on one piece; it does not compound across three.
4. **Feeding Mythic Hero Gear into enhancement.** It is the scarcest material in the chain and enhancement accepts anything.
5. **Ascending before the state allows it and assuming the button is broken.** Ascension is state-gated like everything else. See [Server Timeline](/reference/server-timeline).
6. **Spending Mithril outside a Prep window** when nothing depended on the timing.
7. **Reforging equipped pieces.** Reforge what has been replaced, not what is in use.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Order of the four systems | Enhancement → Mastery Forging → Ascension → Empowerment |
| Mastery gate for Empowerment | **Lv 11**, confirmed on the in-game screen |
| Mastery gate for Ascension | Reported as Lv 10, **unconfirmed** |
| Cost of one empowerment | 10 Mithril + 3 Mythic Hero Gear |
