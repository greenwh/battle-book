# Winter Siege

::: tip
**Quick take:** Monthly alliance-vs-alliance Stronghold battle. Top 20 alliances in your state, Furnace Lv. 25 personal minimum. Each Chief deploys up to two squads on Wednesday and Thursday, everything locks Thursday 23:59 UTC, and Saturday is a single 24-hour Battle Phase. Troops are provided by the system and nothing dies permanently. The whole event is a Star count: your garrisoned squads carry Stars, and you only take an enemy's Stars by destroying a squad completely. Partial damage scores nothing.
:::

## At a Glance

- **Frequency:** Monthly, starting the Monday of Alliance Showdown week
- **Duration:** Seven-day cycle, one 24-hour Battle Phase on Saturday
- **Eligibility:** Alliance must be top 20 by power in the state. Each Chief needs Furnace Lv. 25 and must be in the alliance when matchmaking begins
- **Troop cost:** None. Troops are system-provided and all injuries recover after each battle
- **Why it matters:** Tier promotion rewards, per-tier battle rewards, Stronghold rewards, and improved Win Streak rewards for winning two consecutive events

## How It Works

Two alliances are matched against each other. Each holds a set of Strongholds. Your members garrison squads in your own Strongholds during the Preparation Phase; on Saturday, those same squads defend automatically and can also be sent to attack the enemy's Strongholds.

**The three numbers that run the event:**

| Icon | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Flag** | The number of squads garrisoned in a Stronghold |
| **Star** | Score. Every garrisoned squad contributes Stars to the Stronghold it sits in. The amount varies by Stronghold — check Stronghold Info in-game for the current values |
| **Heart** | A squad's health. A squad loses one Heart every time it loses a defensive battle |

**The rule that shapes everything else:** a squad's Stars are only deducted when it loses **all** of its Hearts. Official example — a squad with 4 Hearts and 6 Stars must fail four defenses; at that fourth loss the Stronghold drops all 6 Stars at once. Take three of its four Hearts and you have scored nothing.

**Why it's distinctive:**

- **No troop losses.** Troops are provided by the system at your highest unlocked troop level and Fire Crystal level for each type. Injured troops on both sides recover after every battle.
- **Everything locks Thursday.** Heroes, formation, troop ratio, Stronghold assignment, and every stat bonus freeze at the end of the Preparation Phase.
- **The same squad attacks and defends.** There is no separate attack lineup. A squad built purely to tank will be a poor attacker, and vice versa.
- **Attacks are a limited budget, not a cooldown.** Each squad gets a fixed number of attacks for the whole Battle Phase.
- **Defenders get weaker as they win.** Morale decays with each successful defense, so numbers can wear down a stronger squad.

### The Seven-Day Cycle

All times UTC.

| Day | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Monday 00:00 – Tuesday 23:00 | Preview | View your alliance's ranking on the event page |
| Tuesday 23:00 – 23:59 | Matchmaking | You must be in your alliance during this hour to participate |
| Wednesday 00:00 – Thursday 23:59 | Preparation | Set squads, heroes, formations, troop ratios, Stronghold assignments, and Wardens |
| Friday 00:00 – 23:59 | Scout | View enemy Stronghold garrisons. No attacks |
| **Saturday 00:00 – 23:59** | **Battle** | **Automatic defense plus your attack budget** |
| Sunday 00:00 – 23:59 | Display | Results and rewards |

### Eligibility and Matchmaking

- **Alliance:** top 20 by power in the state.
- **Personal:** you must be in an eligible alliance when matchmaking begins, and your Furnace must reach **Lv. 25** during the event. If your Furnace is below 25 at matchmaking, you can still join the Stronghold Battle after upgrading it during the event.
- Groups are divided by server progress, and opponents with similar Star Ratings are matched first inside a group.
- A matchmaking **bye (Auto-Advance)** counts as a win. Rewards are settled after the Matchmaking Phase.
- Tier rewards are based on your alliance's tier **at the start of the event**, not the finish.
- A **Win Streak** means winning two consecutive events, and pays better than a single win.
- There are **no ranking rewards** in this event.

## Preparation Phase

### Squad Deployment

Each Chief deploys up to two squads to friendly Strongholds. Two squads may sit in the same Stronghold.

**Garrison capacity is limited.** Each Stronghold accepts a fixed number of squads. When more squads are assigned than the cap allows, the Warden Squad and the highest-power squads get priority and the rest are rejected from the garrison.

::: tip Rejected does not mean benched
A squad that fails to garrison still gets its full attack budget on Saturday. It contributes no Stars and carries no Star risk, but it is a full attacker. In a large alliance, the overflow squads are your offensive reserve, not wasted deployments.
:::

**R4 and R5 can move any member's squads between Strongholds** during Preparation. This is the main leadership lever in the whole event — do not leave placement to whoever logged in first.

**Automatic fallbacks at the start of the Scout Phase:**

- A Stronghold with no garrisoned squads gets one system-generated squad.
- A Stronghold with no appointed Warden has its most powerful squad appointed automatically.

Both fallbacks are worse than a real decision. Neither should ever fire.

### What Counts and What Does Not

These bonuses **are** included in battlefield calculations:

Troops · Hero Levels · Hero Star Levels · Hero Skills · Hero Gear · Hero Exclusive Gear **stats** · global Jeronimo and Natalia bonuses (these apply if you own the hero, even when not deployed) · Pet Skills (active by default; manual activation does not stack) · Pet Stats, Levels, Advancements, Refinements · Chief Charms · Chief Gear · Tech · Fire Crystal Tech · Flame Tech · Alliance Tech · Daybreak Island · Skins · VIP Level · Buildings · Facility (**must be active during squad setup**) · Expert Level, Relationship Advancements, Research, and Skills.

These bonuses **do not** apply:

Hero Exclusive Gear **Skills** · Territory bonuses · State Positions · President Skills · City Bonuses · Frostdragon Tyrant titles.

::: warning Two easy points to miss
Exclusive Gear contributes its stats but not its skill, so an Exclusive Gear skill that reads well on paper does nothing here. And the alliance Facility bonus only counts if it is **active while you set your squad** — leadership should confirm facility buffs are held and running across Wednesday and Thursday, not just on battle day.
:::

Squad power recalculates in real time while the Preparation Phase is open, and the bonuses in effect at the end of that phase are final. If squad capacity changes after deployment, troops are redistributed automatically using the ratio you originally chose.

### Wardens

Each Stronghold can appoint **one Warden Squad**. Warden appointments can be changed at any time during the Preparation Phase.

- **Only the top 5 players by squad power in that Stronghold are eligible.** You cannot appoint a low-power favourite.
- A Warden gains **extra Stars and extra Hearts**.
- Warden marks and Star values differ between Strongholds.

The extra Hearts are what matter tactically. A Warden with more Hearts forces the attacker through more full Morale-grind cycles before it dies — see below. The extra Stars are the price of failure if they get through.

**Practical Warden rules:**

1. One Warden per Stronghold. Never leave the auto-appointment to fire.
2. Prefer a strong squad belonging to a player who will actually be online during the Battle Phase — Wardens still attack, and a Warden's attack budget is as valuable as anyone's.
3. Do not stack every strong player into one Stronghold. Strongholds are connected, and a cheap Stronghold is a doorway.
4. Rank is not a qualification. Appoint by squad power, not by title.

## Battle Phase

### The Attack Budget

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting attempts per squad | 3 |
| Restore rate | 1 every 2 hours |
| Maximum restored per squad | 7 |
| **Total per squad for the whole phase** | **10** |
| Total per Chief (two squads) | 20 |

Attempts **bank**. You can save them and spend them all at once, which is what makes coordinated timed waves possible. An alliance with 40 participants has roughly 800 attacks available across the day — that is the resource leadership is actually allocating.

Restoration completes about 14 hours into the 24-hour phase, so late-day attacking draws on banked attempts rather than fresh ones.

### The Attack Path

Strongholds are connected. **You must defeat every garrison squad in a Stronghold before you can attack the Strongholds connected beyond it.** This is the same gating idea as Canyon Clash: the map is a network, not a menu.

Two consequences:

- **Your weakest Stronghold is your front door.** A thin outer garrison is not a small loss, it is an opening to everything behind it.
- **Clearing a Stronghold means killing every squad in it**, not damaging them. There is no partial progress toward opening a path.

### Morale

Officially: **Squad Stats = Squad Stats at the end of the Preparation Phase × Morale.**

Community testing during the first runs of the event documents the decay schedule below. Treat the exact percentages as a working model and confirm them against the in-game display.

- Attacker Morale stays fixed at 100%.
- A **garrison** squad loses Morale each time it **wins** a defense.

| Current Morale | Drop per successful defense |
|---|---|
| Above 70% | 10 percentage points |
| Above 35% up to 70% | 5 percentage points |
| 35% or below | No further drop (floor) |

Following that schedule, a garrison squad's Morale runs 100 → 90 → 80 → 70 → 65 → 60 → 55 → 50 → 45 → 40 → 35, reaching the floor after **ten successful defenses**.

**Morale resets to 100% when the squad loses a Heart but survives.**

::: danger The kill cycle is the core arithmetic of the event
Because Morale resets on every Heart lost, each Heart is a fresh grind. To take a squad with four Hearts you must wear its Morale down four separate times. Attacks that lose are not wasted — they are the mechanism. This is why a coordinated alliance of ordinary accounts can remove a heavily invested defender that no single member could beat.
:::

### Winning

1. If one alliance defeats **all** defending squads in the enemy's Strongholds and reduces the enemy's Stronghold Stars to zero first, it wins immediately.
2. Otherwise, at the end of the Battle Phase, the side with **more remaining Stronghold Stars** wins.
3. If Star counts are equal, the side that **reached that count later** wins.
4. **Special case:** if both sides had equal Stars before the Battle Phase and still have equal Stars after it, **both sides lose.**

Rule 4 is the one people get caught by. A quiet Saturday against an evenly matched opponent is not a draw — it is a double loss. Doing nothing is never the safe option.

## Strategy

### Every Player

- **Deploy both squads during Preparation.** They are your Stars and your entire attack budget.
- **Build squads that can attack and defend**, because they will do both. A pure tank wastes ten attacks; a glass cannon folds on defense.
- **Finish every upgrade before Thursday 23:59 UTC.** Gear, charms, pets, and expert progress all count, and all freeze at that moment.
- **Check that the alliance Facility bonus is active when you set your squad.**
- **Spend your attacks.** Ten per squad is generous and they do not carry to next month.

### Troop Ratio

Whiteout Survival does not publish an official best ratio for this event, and the squad you register has to survive defense and be useful on offense. The widely used all-purpose PvP starting point is a reasonable default if you have not tested your own:

| Purpose | Infantry / Lancers / Marksmen | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| General all-purpose (default) | 50 / 20 / 30 | Balanced durability and damage |
| Leaning attack | 50 / 10 / 40 or 45 / 20 / 35 | For overflow squads that will never garrison |
| Leaning defense | 60 / 20 / 20 | Only if the squad is certain to garrison and you accept weaker attacks |
| Marksman-heavy PvE ratios | Not recommended | Bear Trap ratios are not PvP defense ratios |

Because the ratio locks with everything else, avoid a pure tank build for any squad that also has to attack. See [Troop Ratios](/troops/ratios) for the underlying mechanics.

### R4 / R5 Coordinator

- **Placement is your job, not the members'.** You can move every squad. Do it before Thursday closes.
- **Fill Stronghold capacity with your strongest squads first**, and let the overflow become a deliberate attack reserve.
- **Appoint every Warden manually.** Eligibility is capped to the top 5 squads by power in that Stronghold, so choose from that pool and prefer players who will be online Saturday.
- **Do not leave a Stronghold thin because it looks unimportant.** The path rule turns it into an entrance.
- **Use the Scout Phase properly.** Friday is a full day to read every enemy garrison, count Hearts, and decide the target order before anyone can attack.
- **Concentrate attacks.** Assign specific targets in a specific order and hold members to them.
- **Strike early.** Rules 3 and 4 above both reward removing enemy Stars sooner rather than later.

### Target Selection

The maths follows directly from the Star rule.

- **Focus fire always beats spreading.** Twenty attacks that destroy one squad take its Stars. Twenty attacks spread across five squads take nothing.
- **Count Hearts before committing.** A squad's cost is roughly its Heart count multiplied by a full Morale grind. Budget the whole kill before you start it, or you will spend attacks for zero score.
- **Cheap squads first when you need a path open**, because opening a Stronghold requires clearing all of it. High-Star squads first when you are racing the Star count.
- **A Warden is the most expensive target on the board and the highest payout.** Do not start one you cannot finish.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Damaging squads instead of killing them.** Stars only move when a squad loses every Heart. Half a kill is worth exactly zero.

2. **Spreading attacks across a Stronghold.** Five squads at half health at the end of the day is the worst possible outcome for the same number of attacks.

3. **Assuming the enemy Warden is unbeatable.** Morale decay means it gets weaker every time it wins. Ordinary squads are the grind; the strong squads land the finish.

4. **Registering a pure defense build.** The same squad has ten attacks to spend. A 60/30/10 tank throws them away.

5. **Missing the Thursday lock.** Everything freezes at 23:59 UTC Thursday. An upgrade finished Friday morning contributes nothing.

6. **Forgetting the Facility bonus must be live during squad setup.** It is the one bonus with a timing condition attached.

7. **Building around Hero Exclusive Gear skills.** The stats count. The skills do not.

8. **Letting the auto-Warden fire.** The system picks the highest-power squad in the Stronghold, which is often not the player who will be awake on Saturday.

9. **Leaving an outer Stronghold thin.** Strongholds gate each other. A cheap one to clear is a route to the expensive ones.

10. **Sitting on attack attempts until they expire.** Restoration finishes about 14 hours in, and unspent attempts are gone at the end of the phase.

11. **Playing passively while tied.** Equal Stars before and after the Battle Phase means **both** alliances lose.

12. **Chasing a State Title or President skill for the event.** State Positions, President Skills, City Bonuses, Territory bonuses, and Frostdragon Tyrant titles are all excluded.
